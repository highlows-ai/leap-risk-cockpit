LEAP RISK COCKPIT — v0.1 (ASCII Edition)

REGIMES:
  Calm:      low IV, stable rates, long time → LEAP ≈ stock
  Volatile:  high IV, stable rates, long time → vega-dominated
  Rate:      low IV, rising rates → forward-tilt
  Compress:  high IV, falling rates → forward-collapse
  Late:      short time → gamma-blowout

PIPES:
  Spot | IV | Rates | Time | Forward | Gamma | Dealer Hedging | Liquidity | Skew

PANELS:
  Delta Surface:  time × moneyness
  Gamma Surface:  time × moneyness
  Vega Surface:   time × moneyness

NOTES:
  - Deep ITM + long time → synthetic stock zone
  - ATM + long time → convexity zone
  - ATM + short time → gamma instability zone
  - High IV → delta lies
  - IV crush → LEAP diverges from stock

IV CRUSH PANEL
-----------------------------------------
| Regime Transition   | IV Move | Price |
-----------------------------------------
| volatile → calm     |   ↓↓↓   |  ↑↑   |
| compress → calm     |   ↓↓↓   |  ↑↑↑  |
| rate → calm         |   ↓     |  ↑    |
| panic → calm (meta) |   ↓↓↓   |  ↑    |
-----------------------------------------
Notes:
- Rallies reduce uncertainty → IV falls.
- IV falls → extrinsic value collapses.
- Short-dated options suffer the worst crush.
- LEAPS dampen crush but do not eliminate it.

IV SPIKE PANEL
-----------------------------------------
| Regime Transition   | IV Move | Price |
-----------------------------------------
| calm → volatile     |   ↑↑↑   |  ↓↓   |
| calm → compress     |   ↑↑↑   |  ↓↓↓  |
| calm → rate         |   ↑     |  ↓    |
-----------------------------------------
Notes:
- Dips increase uncertainty → IV rises.
- IV rises → extrinsic expands.
- Short-dated options inflate fastest.
- LEAPS benefit from IV spikes during dips.

GAMMA ACCELERATION PANEL
-----------------------------------------
| Time to Expiry | Gamma Level | Behavior        |
-----------------------------------------
| > 60 days      |   Low       | Stable          |
| 30–60 days     |   Medium    | Rising          |
| < 30 days      |   High      | Blowout Zone    |
-----------------------------------------
Notes:
- Gamma accelerates as expiry approaches.
- Delta becomes unstable in the blowout zone.
- Short-dated options behave like leveraged spot.
- LEAPS avoid gamma blowout entirely.
