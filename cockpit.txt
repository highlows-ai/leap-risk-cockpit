  Volatile:  high IV, stable rates, long time → vega-dominated
  Rate:      low IV, rising rates → forward-tilt
  Compress:  high IV, falling rates → forward-collapse
  Late:      short time → gamma-blowout

NOTES:
  - Deep ITM + long time → synthetic stock zone
  - ATM + long time → convexity zone
  - ATM + short time → gamma instability zone
  - High IV → delta lies
  - IV crush → LEAP diverges from stock

DIVIDEND IMPACT PANEL
-----------------------------------------
| Metric             | Behavior          |
-----------------------------------------
| dividend_yield     | reduces_call_value |
| forward_adjustment | lowers_forward_price |
| delta_adjustment   | reduces_delta      |
| synthetic_drag     | reduces_synthetic_exposure |
| put_skew           | increases_put_value |
-----------------------------------------
Notes:
- Dividends reduce call value because the stock drops on ex-div.
- Forward price adjusts downward by PV(dividends).
- Delta decreases because calls become less stock-like.
- Synthetic exposure drifts lower unless adjusted.
- Puts gain value, increasing put skew.

Dividends reduce the forward price.
Lower forward compresses crash-wing IV.
Lower crash-wing IV reduces catastrophic downside.
Lower catastrophic downside stabilizes gamma.
Stable gamma stabilizes delta.
Stable delta stabilizes synthetic exposure.
